Activity: The 9 Dot Challenge

Connect all nine dots using four straight lines without lifting your mouse.

How to play
Press and hold on any dot, then drag one continuous path. Draw naturally. The game automatically straightens your stroke into line segments as you move. You can draw horizontally, vertically, diagonally, and beyond the outside dots. Do not release until you are finished.
Tip: you do not need a perfectly steady hand. The activity smooths your stroke into straight lines while preserving your turns.

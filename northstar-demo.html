Travel Request Quick Guide

Before You Begin

  • Obtain approval before booking overnight travel.
  • Book through the Northstar Travel Portal.
  • Confirm the hotel rate for your destination.

What You Need

  • Business purpose and destination
  • Travel dates
  • Estimated airfare, lodging, meals, and registration
  • Cost center and approval status

Expense Limits

Breakfast$20
Lunch$25
Dinner$45
Denver hotel maximum$250/night
Mileage$0.70/mile

Common Mistakes

  • Booking before approval
  • Entering only the event name as the business purpose
  • Leaving registration fees out of the estimate
  • Submitting expenses more than 10 business days later

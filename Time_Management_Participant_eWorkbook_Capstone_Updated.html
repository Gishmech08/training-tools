Your top time drains

Identify your top three time drains. Classify each as Control, Influence, or Adapt. Then compare your choices with a partner.

Expected output: three real time drains and a classification for each.
Time drainClassificationWhy?
Control: you can change your own behavior, approach, or system. Influence: change requires a conversation or agreement with someone else. Adapt: the demand is a legitimate part of the job, so plan around it.

Reference: Track before you fix

A short time audit can help you compare planned time, actual time, interruptions, and the value of the work. The course recommends using your own evidence before changing your system.

Manage multiple priorities

Use explicit tradeoffs when everything cannot be priority one.

Priority Filter

  1. What outcome does this support?
  2. What happens if it waits?
  3. Who depends on it?
  4. What is the deadline?
  5. What must move if I take it on?

Recognize overload early

Identify the overload signal that tends to show up first for you. Then choose one course strategy you can use when you notice it.

Pressure Reset

1. StopPause long enough to see what changed.
2. ReassessIdentify the new facts, deadlines, and dependencies.
3. ReprioritizeDecide what rises, moves, or no longer fits.
4. CommunicateTell the people affected by the changed plan.
5. RestartChoose one clear next action.

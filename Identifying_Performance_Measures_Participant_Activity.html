Identifying Performance Measures

In groups of 3–4, identify 3–5 key performance metrics used in your area, then prepare to share out.

How It Works

1
Brainstorm
Identify the metrics your team relies on most.
2
Capture
Add 3–5 metrics to your group's sticky-note board.
3
Share
Choose one metric to discuss with the full class.

Examples to Get You Thinking

Use these only as prompts. Your group's metrics should reflect your own work area.

On-Time Performance (OTP)Schedule adherence and reliability
Mean Distance Between Failures (MDBF)Vehicle reliability and maintenance effectiveness
Ridership RecoveryPassenger volumes compared with historical baselines
Customer Incident ReportsSafety and customer experience

The Process in Plain Language

1. RequestTell us what is needed.
2. TriageDetermine ownership and the best path.
3. PlanConfirm approach, people, timing and resources.
4. Do the WorkDevelop, modify, procure or reuse.
5. Review & ReleaseValidate, approve and release.
6. SustainMaintain, measure and improve.

Admin: Intake Rules

New trainingRoute to Develop
Existing training needs changesRoute to Modify

Admin: Minimum Required Information

The NeedProblem, audience, desired result
The WorkExisting assets, likely solution type
The PeopleRequestor, owner, SMEs, colleges
ConstraintsTiming, funding, technology, risks
SustainmentOwner, review date, measures
